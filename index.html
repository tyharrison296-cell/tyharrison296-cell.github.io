Discord Checks Guide
  • Always perform a voice channel check with all players and a text channel check.
  • Ensure all members have their Discord account linked and verified.
  • Confirm the user has read and accepted all server rules.
  • Check for inappropriate content in usernames or profiles.
  • Respond politely and professionally to all questions or issues.

  • 1. Are They in the Discord Server & VC?
    • Use the command /erlc membercheck.
    • This pulls all in-game players and shows their Discord status.
    • It will show:
      • If they are in the Discord
      • If they are in a Voice Channel (VC)
    • This works through Central or Melonly.
    • Use Melonly as the primary tool.
    • If Melonly is down, switch to Central.

  • 2. If Melonly or Central Are Down Completely:
    • You may teleport to the player ONLY to bring them to the Staff Base.
    • Ask if they are in the Discord and in a VC (if required).
    • If they are not, or they lie/refuse to answer:
      • Jail if under 39/40
      • Kick if 39/40

  • Stay professional and consistent.
  • These checks are for staff use only; abuse will not be tolerated.

Requirements & Policies

Minimum Hours: You are expected to complete 4 hours every week on shift using Melonly. You have one full week to complete this quota.

Leave of Absence (LOA): If you are unable to meet your required hours, you must submit an LOA. Valid reasons include mental or health issues, school exams, holidays, and other reasonable circumstances.

Promotions:
• Single Promotion: 6 hours (2 logs per hour)
• Double Promotion: 14 hours (2 logs per hour)
• Triple Promotion: 30 hours (2 logs per hour)

Strike Policy: Receiving 2 or more strikes during a promotion work period will disqualify you from promotion eligibility for that week.

Infraction Policy: All infractions are not appealable until their expiry timer has passed.

Staff of the Week: The staff member with the highest total hours for the week will be selected as Staff of the Week.

Retiring Policy: Staff below the rank of JA will not receive the “Retired Staff” role. Staff ranked TIA or higher are eligible for the “Retired High Rank” role.

Reinstatement Policy: Prior TIA+ members may return as a maximum rank of Junior Moderator. Members with the “Retired Staff” role (JA+) may return as Trial Staff.

Staff Outposts

Staff Outposts are designated supervision areas used to maintain server order and monitor high-activity locations. Outposts are ranked from most important (1) to least important (4). You must prioritize higher-ranked outposts first.

While stationed at an outpost, you must be actively performing moderation duties such as Discord checks, voice checks, or handling nearby rule violations.

Outpost Rules:

  • A maximum of 2 on-duty staff members may occupy one outpost.
  • You must remain in your vehicle while at an outpost.
  • Do not make it obvious that the location is a staff base.
  • You may load users only if they harass or interfere with your duties.

Duties While at an Outpost

  1. Monitor roleplay activity in and around your assigned area.
  2. Conduct Discord and VC checks regularly.
  3. Respond to nearby rule-breaking immediately.
  4. Handle mod calls and return to your outpost afterward.

What You Should NOT Do

  1. Never stand idle — always be moderating or checking activity.
  2. Never interfere with ongoing roleplay unless rules are broken.
  3. Never assist in police chases unless moderation is required.

Outpost Locations

Outpost 1 – Spawn

The highest-priority outpost. Spawn experiences the most trolling, RDM, and VDM. Constant supervision is required.

Outpost 2 – Gun Store

High RDM risk due to weapon access. Expect frequent moderation logs and incidents.

Outpost 3 – Mod Shop

Responsible for monitoring the nearby intersection, fire station, and bank. Staff must stay alert for gunshots and fail roleplay.

Outpost 4 – Three Guys

Lowest activity outpost. Focus on Discord and VC checks while monitoring the city center, jewelry store, and surrounding areas.

Your priority at all outposts is supervision and moderation. Always remain professional, active, and alert.
